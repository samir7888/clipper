Frequently asked questions

Is Clip Machine free to use?

Yes. Paste a stream link or upload a VOD and generate clips at no cost.

Does it work with live streams?

Yes. Paste your stream link before you go live, and it records automatically until you stop or the stream ends.

Do I need video editing skills?

No editing is required. Highlights are detected and reformatted for vertical video automatically.

Will clips include my facecam?

If a facecam is detected, clips are automatically laid out with gameplay on top and the facecam zoomed in below, matching the format used on Shorts and TikTok.
